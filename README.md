# The Book That Fought Back

The night the sky broke open, a colossal octopus coiled around the city's skyscrapers, dragging its tentacles through glowing fractures in the heavens. On a deserted rooftop, the city's forgotten spellbook opened itself against the wind. From its golden pages rose a tornado of a thousand silver swords, each carrying a promise the city had once written down: when the monster came, something would answer.
